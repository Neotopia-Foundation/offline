# Code of conduct

This project is open to everyone who wants to help people in Europe prepare for a crisis. We want every contributor to feel safe here.

## What we expect
- Be respectful, also when you disagree.
- Criticise the work, not the person.
- Accept corrections to facts and sources. The guide must be correct, so we welcome corrections.
- Help new contributors.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal data about other people.

## What we do not accept
- Insults, harassment, threats and personal attacks.
- Sexual language or sexual images.
- Discrimination because of origin, nationality, language, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability or any other personal trait.
- Publication of private information about another person without their permission.
- False information that you add on purpose to the guide, to issues or to discussions.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this code applies
This code applies in all spaces of the Neotopia Foundation on GitHub:
- repositories,
- issues and pull requests,
- discussions.

It also applies when you represent the foundation in public.

## How to report a problem
Send an email to contact@neotopia.foundation. Write what happened, where it happened and when. Add links or screenshots if you have them.
- We read every report.
- We keep the name of the person who reports private.
- Do not report conduct problems in a public issue.

For a security problem, use the steps in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What we can do
The maintainers decide what to do. Possible actions:
1. A private warning.
2. A temporary ban from the project spaces.
3. A permanent ban.

We remove content that breaks this code.
