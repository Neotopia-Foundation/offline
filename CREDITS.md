# Credits

**Lastbook** by Immortal Protocols gave us the idea: an offline library for the time when the internet is gone. Lastbook is an open project under the MIT licence: https://github.com/Immortal-Protocols/lastbook. Neotopia Offline is an independent project of the Neotopia Foundation for Europe. It is not an official Lastbook edition. It does not use Lastbook code.

The guide points to the work of many publishers. Thank you to:
- Kiwix and openZIM, for the ZIM libraries and readers.
- OpenStreetMap contributors, Geofabrik and OsmAnd, for maps.
- The Copernicus programme (DLR, Airbus), ESA WorldCover and GeoNames, for terrain, land cover and place names.
- Overture Maps Foundation, for open address data.
- The civil-protection agencies of the European countries, for their preparedness guides.
- The European Commission, for the 72-hour self-sufficiency guidelines.
- Wikimedia, Project Gutenberg, LibriVox and Wolne Lektury, for knowledge, books and audiobooks.
- llama.cpp, Google Gemma and Unsloth, for the local AI model and its runtime.
- The authors of all open-source programs named in the guide.

Reviews: two AI models (OpenAI Codex and Google Gemini) reviewed the guide on 2026-11-21. Every finding was checked before it went into the text.
